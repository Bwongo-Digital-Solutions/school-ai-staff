The staff companion to a school's e-School system. A teacher, matron, bursar, cook or
gate keeper scans a student's ID card and sees exactly the part of that student's record
their job needs — and nothing else.

What a scan shows is decided by the server and shaped by the role, so a gate keeper's
screen carries no fees and a cook's carries no contact details.

Features
* Scan a student ID card with the camera, or search by name
* Marks: record and review, by class and subject
* Fees: balances and payment history, for the roles that handle money
* Dormitories: beds and roll call, for the matron
* Gate: passes, check in and check out, with the day's movement log
* Roll call: call the register for a class, or scan cards at the door
* Exam clearance: granted by a bursar, checked at the door by an invigilator
* Notifications: staff messages and school events
* Assistant: chat and search over school data, for administrators and teachers
* Dark and light themes

This app is a client. It holds no school records of its own and asks for a login before
it shows anything. It needs the address of your school's own server, which you set on the
sign-in screen; your school's administrator will give you that address, and usually a QR
code that fills it in for you.

Built for schools that cannot rely on a fast connection or a Play Store account: the
server can be on the school's own network, and the app is distributed as a plain APK.
